# Potato Casserole

Recipe via Grandma Paparone.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb package frozen, shredded hashed brown potatoes, thawed
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 cup cheddar cheese, grated
- 1/2 cup diced onions
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 1 ca cream of chicken soup
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 350°F.
2. **Prepare:** Grease 9x13 greased casserole baking dish.
3. **Mix:** In a large bowl, combine ingredients.
4. **Fill:** Pour the mixture into the casserole dish. Top with 1 1/2 cup crushed rice Chex and 1/4 cup melted butter
5. **Bake:** 45-50 minutes or until bubbly.
