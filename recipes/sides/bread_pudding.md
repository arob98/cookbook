# Bread Pudding

Recipe via Grandma Paparone.

## Ingredients and Instructions

Preheat to 350. 

1 1/2 loaf of bread - lightly trim crust and quarter pieces of bread.
Arrange in dish. Sprinkle with 3/4 c reg raisins and 3/4 c golden raisins. 

Break and blend 4 eggs and 4 egg yolks. Add following ingredients to the eggs:  
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 tsp orange zest
- 1 tsp lemon zest

In a saucepan, heat 1 3/4 cup cream and 1 3/4 milk. 

Take off heat. Add to eggs very slowly. 

Spoon over the bread and raisins. Sprinkle with 1/2 tsp cinnamon and 2 tbsp sugar.

Bake 35 minutes until set. Cool for 30 minutes. Serve.
