# Autumn Salad

Recipe via Grandma Paparone.

## Ingredients

- 2 large red delicious apples
- 1/4 cup walnuts
- 1/3 cup blue cheese
- romaine lettuce

### Dressing
- 1 tsp dijon mustard
- 1/4 c white whine vinegar
- 1/8 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp dill weed
- 1/2 tsp nutmeg

Slowly add 1/2 cup olive oil.
