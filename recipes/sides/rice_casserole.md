# Rice Casserole

Recipe via Grandma Paparone.

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 2 small cans of mushrooms
- 2 8-oz can of beef consume
- 1 1/3 cup uncooked rice
- 1 onion, chopped 

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 350°F.
2. **Prepare:** Grease 9x13 greased casserole baking dish.
3. **Mix:** Saute onion in butter. Add mushrooms, consume to heat. Add rice. 
4. **Fill:** Pour the mixture into the casserole dish.
5. **Bake:** 60 minutes.
