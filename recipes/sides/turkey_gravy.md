# Turkey Gravy

Recipe via Grandma Paparone.

## Ingredients

- 3 cup stock
- 2 tbsp flour
- 6 tbsp drippings

## Instructions

Warm drippings in sauce pan. Add flour. Whisk. Add hot stock. Whisk vigorously. Add a additional drippings as needed for liquid.
