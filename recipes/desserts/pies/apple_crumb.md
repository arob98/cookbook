# Apple crumb pie

Grandma Paparone recipe

## Ingredients

- pie crust
- cut up 6-8 peeled Granny Smith apples
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 3/4 tsp cinnamon
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 3/4 cup flour
- 6 tbsp butter

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 400°F.
2. **Prepare the pan:** Grease pie pan.
3. **Mix:** Place the eggs, sugar, cinnamon mixture in the pie crust. Mix and sprinkle sugar, flour, and butter over the apple mixture.
4. **Bake:** Bake for 35-40 minutes.
