# Date Cake

Recipe via Grandma Paparone.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup dates
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 cup boiling water
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 egg
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, sifted
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- powdered sugar

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 350°F.
2. **Prepare:** Grease and flour loaf pan.
3. **Mix:** Cut dates. Mix with pecans. Mix baking soda with boiling water and pour over dates and pecans. Cream sugar with butter. Beat in egg. Mix and sift dry ingredients. Mix with egg mixture. Stir in date - pecan mixture.
4. **Add:** Pour mixture into pan.
6. **Bake:** 25 minutes 

Sprinkle with powdered sugar and serve.
