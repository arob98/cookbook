# Spiced Baked Doughnuts

Recipe via Grandma Paparone.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 1/2 cup plus 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup 1% low-fat milk
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 1 egg
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- powdered sugar

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 400°F.
2. **Prepare:** Grease mini muffin pan.
3. **Mix:** Combine first 6 ingredients in a large bowl. Make a well in the center of the mixture. Combine milk and next 3 ingredients. Stri well. Add to dry ingredenients, stirring just until moistened. 
4. **Add:** Spoon batter by 2 heaping tablespoons into each cup of a mini muffin pan. Score tops evenly with knife.
4. **Bake:** 12 min or until wooden toothpiucks in center come out clean. 

Sprinkle with powdered sugar and serve. 24 mini muffins.
