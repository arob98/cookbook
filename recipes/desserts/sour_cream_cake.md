# Sour cream cake

Grandma Paparone recipe

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs, separated
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 1/2 cup flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup chopped pecans

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 350°F.
2. **Prepare the pan:** Grease and flour 9-in tube pan. 
3. **Mix:** Beat egg yolks well, add sugar, sour cream, and vanilla thoroughly. Sift together flour, soda, baking powder, salt. Add pecans to sour cream mixture. Stir only until no more flour is visible. Fold in slightly beaten egg whites.
4. **Fill the pan:** Pour into prepared pan.
5. **Bake:** Bake for 50 minutes.
