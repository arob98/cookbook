# Chocolate chip cookies

This recipe makes a pan of cookies (not drop). 

## Ingredients

- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup (1 sticks) butter, softened
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 unbeaten egg
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 375°F.
2. **Prepare the pan:** Grease 15 x 10-inch jelly-roll pan. 
3. **Mix:** Blend dry ingredients. Mix in wet ingredients. Shape into dough balls.
4. **Ready for baking:** Roll in sugar and place on cookie sheet.
5. **Bake:** Bake for 8 minutes or until golden brown. Place chocolate in center + press down. \

Makes 3 dozen cookies.
