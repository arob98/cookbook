# Pumpkin muffins

Salem Tavern recipe. 

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ginger
- 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/8 tsp ground cloves
- 1/3 cup raisins
- 1/3 cup soft butter
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 1/4 cup molasses
- 2 beaten eggs
- 1 cup canned pumpkin
- 1/2 cup milk

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 375°F.
2. **Prepare the pan:** Grease muffin pan. 
3. **Mix:** Sift together flour, baking powder, salt, spices. Add raisins and coat. Cream sugar, butter, and molasses; add milk, eggs, and pumpkin. Blend well. Stir in dry ingredients blending only until flour disappears.
4. **Ready for baking:** Fill greased muffin pans full.
5. **Bake:** Bake for 16-18 min. 

Makes 12-15 large muffins.
