# Date Cake

Recipe via Grandma Paparone.

## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup molasses
- 1/3 cup sour cream
- 1 egg, slightly beaten
- 1 cup sifted flour
- 1/3 tsp baking soda
- 1/3 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1/4 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 375°F.
2. **Prepare:** Grease and flour loaf pan.
3. **Mix:** Cream butter, add sugar, molasses, sour cream, and egg. Sift flour with remaining ingredients and stir into creamed mixture.
4. **Add:** Pour mixture into pan.
6. **Bake:** 25 minutes 

Sprinkle with powdered sugar and serve.
